Model answer: “At Mercado Libre the infrastructure of the compute platform lived in AWS and GCP, provisioned with Terraform. I worked on three things. Shared modules: self-service modules and templates that other teams used to get infrastructure without opening a ticket to us. State management: remote state with locking — S3 with locking in AWS, GCS in GCP — split by environment and account, so the blast radius of a mistake is small and plans run fast. And migrations: moving infrastructure from one mechanism to another, which means bringing existing resources under Terraform with import, checking that the plan shows no changes, and only then switching ownership.” Likely follow-ups: “How do you split state?” — by environment, account or region, and by layer (network, cluster, apps). “How do you refactor without destroying?” — moved blocks or terraform state mv. “How do you import?” — import blocks (Terraform 1.5+) or terraform import, then plan must be clean.
