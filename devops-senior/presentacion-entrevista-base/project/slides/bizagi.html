At Bizagi the challenge was to build the company’s Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery. First CI: every change was built and went through automated tests inside the pipeline, in Azure Pipelines and Jenkins — if a test failed, the change didn’t move forward. Then CD: versioning, and promotion through Dev, Test, UAT (User Acceptance Testing) and Production, and customers could choose when to upgrade. The platform was certified under healthcare compliance standards, so every version had to be traceable. I also automated the onboarding of new customers: Terraform and ARM Templates as infrastructure as code, Python and PowerShell to provision Azure resources, Kubernetes on Azure, and every customer got Grafana dashboards for health and logs from day one. I was also on call supporting production.
