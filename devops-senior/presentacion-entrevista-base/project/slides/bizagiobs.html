Model answer: “At Bizagi I led, end to end, the observability part of customer onboarding. When a new customer came in, the infrastructure was provisioned as code, and the same automation created that customer’s Grafana dashboards — health and logs — plus the alert rules. Those alerts went to the on-call rotation, because we supported production. The result: no customer went live without visibility, and since every customer used the same template, support could compare one customer with another very quickly.
