AWS troubleshooting and access

S3 versioning won’t enable

s3:PutBucketVersioning denied (IAM, SCP, boundary), MFA Delete, Terraform aws_s3_bucket_versioning; suspended, never disabled

AccessDenied on sts:AssumeRole

Two sides must agree: identity policy + trust policy. aws sts get-caller-identity, then CloudTrail

AWS permissions for a Pod

IRSA (IAM Roles for Service Accounts) or EKS Pod Identity — never access keys in a Secret

Public vs private subnet

Public routes 0.0.0.0/0 to the Internet Gateway; private routes it to a NAT Gateway

AssumeRole, out loud: “AssumeRole needs two sides to agree: the caller’s identity policy must allow sts:AssumeRole on the role’s ARN (Amazon Resource Name), and the role’s trust policy must trust the caller. First I confirm who I really am with aws sts get-caller-identity — very often it’s the wrong profile. Then the ARN, the trust policy’s Principal and conditions like ExternalId or MFA, and guardrails like an SCP (Service Control Policy) or a permissions boundary. CloudTrail shows the denied call. With IRSA the call is AssumeRoleWithWebIdentity, and the usual culprit is the sub condition not matching system:serviceaccount:namespace:name.” S3: “Once enabled, versioning can be suspended but never disabled. If it won’t enable, it’s usually permissions or Terraform: since AWS provider v4, versioning is a separate resource, aws_s3_bucket_versioning.” ES: La última fila (subred pública vs privada) conecta con la pregunta de salida a internet desde un Pod.
