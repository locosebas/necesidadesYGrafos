How I handle a production incident

01

Triage

Acknowledge the alert, confirm it is real, size the blast radius, set the severity

02

Mitigate

Stop the bleeding first: rollback, failover, scale out, feature flag off

03

Diagnose

What changed? Golden signals, logs, traces; test one hypothesis at a time

04

Resolve and verify

Ship the real fix; metrics back within the SLO confirm it, not intuition

05

Learn

Blameless postmortem with action items, owners and dates

Communicate the whole time: incident channel, updates every 15–30 minutes on a SEV1, status page — and escalate early.

Model answer: “I follow five steps, and I communicate during all of them. One, triage: I acknowledge the alert so escalation stops, confirm on the dashboard that it’s real, estimate the blast radius — one customer, one region, everything? — and set the severity; when in doubt, the higher one. Two, mitigate: restoring service comes before understanding the cause. If there was a deploy ten minutes before the errors started, I roll back first and investigate later; other options are failover, scaling out or turning off a feature flag. Three, diagnose: what changed, the golden signals — latency, traffic, errors, saturation —, logs and traces, one hypothesis at a time. Four, resolve and verify: the real fix, and the metrics back within the SLO (Service Level Objective) tell me it’s fixed. Five, learn: a blameless postmortem focused on what failed in the system, with action items that have owners and dates.” ES: Frase para recordar el orden: "Clasifico, freno, entiendo, arreglo, aprendo — y aviso todo el tiempo." Error típico: saltar de 1 a 3 sin mitigar.
